What if an Earth dashboard could tell you not only **where** something is happening—but also what is known, what was calculated, and what remains unknown?

That question led to **Terra Pulse**, a living Earth intelligence dashboard that brings fragmented public signals into one operational view.

It connects six live feeds covering earthquakes, volcano alerts, wildfires, severe weather, floods, air quality, and drought. Select an event and Terra Pulse adds source attribution, freshness, weather, nearby-city and hospital context, an evolving timeline, and a relationship graph.

But the most important part is the boundary around AI.

Deterministic code builds the evidence and calculates monitoring priority first. AI is downstream: it explains the structured findings in natural language, cannot create unsupported impact claims, and falls back to the deterministic answer if the model is unavailable.
